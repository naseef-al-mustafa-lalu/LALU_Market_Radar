Signals connecting…

A practice that has merged into another or closed usually stays Active on the NHS directory for a long time but has no list size: a merged practice's patients are counted under the practice that absorbed it, and a closed practice's are re-registering elsewhere. The directory records neither, so we suggest the likely cases (a practice at the same postcode, or a practice deregistered from CQC with nothing to absorb it). Nothing changes until you decide. Confirmed cases are hidden from the main practice list, and a confirmed closure raises one signal on its PCN listing the nearest practices.
